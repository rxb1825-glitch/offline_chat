Connecting…

Connect your phone

First join the laptop's hotspot or the same Wi-Fi network. Then scan this chat QR with your phone's camera and tap the link.

For a laptop hotspot: Windows Settings → Network & internet → Mobile hotspot. Join its network on your phone. If the phone says “No internet”, stay connected.

Choose the adapter for your network. For a Windows hotspot, look for a virtual or “Local Area Connection” adapter. Addresses vary.

The QR opens the chat; it does not join Wi-Fi. Codes are generated on this laptop without internet. Windows may require an available connection to enable its hotspot; a router without internet also works.
